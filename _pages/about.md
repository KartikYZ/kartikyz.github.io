Hi! I'm a software engineer at Citadel. I'm interested in ML research and distributed systems. In my spare time, I like to build things using code, read topics in ML research, and learn about topics in math.

I graduated recently with an MS CS at <b><strong>Georgia Tech</strong></b>. My degree specializations are Machine Learning and Systems. I graduated with a BS CS in December, 2023 from Georgia Tech as well. 

My current research centers around LLM serving of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models and Graph-enabled Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG). I'm currently advised by <a href="https://www.anand-iyer.com/">Dr. Anand Iyer</a> and mentored by <a href="https://mingyuguan.github.io/">Mingyu Guan</a> and <a href="https://https://vimagupta.github.io/">Vima Gupta</a>.

Prior to this I was a research assistant at the Embedded Pervasive Lab. My research centers around scheduling DAG workloads in edge environments and FaaS systems for latency-sensitive/critical applications. I was advised by <a href="https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~rama/">Dr. Ramachandran</a> and mentored by <a href="http://jinsunyoo.com/">Jinsun Yoo</a>.

During university, I interned as a software engineer at Citadel, AWS Cryptography, and Amazon Finance. While on campus, I greatly enjoyed being a TA for CS 3510, spanning algorithm design and analysis, and CS 2110, an introduction to computer architecture and systems.

I also play the piano and enjoy listening to classical, neoclassical and electronic music.
